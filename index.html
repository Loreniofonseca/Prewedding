Our Story

Our story began with a beautiful coincidence in 2024. Antonio, who was working on a construction project, and Maria, who had just returned from her studies abroad, met at a church community event.

From casual conversations, a feeling of mutual respect and admiration slowly grew. Antonio was captivated by Maria’s sincere smile and loving heart, while Maria fell in love with Antonio’s strong faith and hard work.

Through many prayers together, laughter, journeys, and small challenges, God strengthened our hearts that we were created for each other. After two years of walking together, we are certain that this is the time God has appointed for us to make a sacred covenant before Him and in front of our family and friends.

"Not by coincidence, but by grace.
Not just love, but a calling."
